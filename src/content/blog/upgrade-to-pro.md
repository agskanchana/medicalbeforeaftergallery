The free [Medical Before After Gallery](https://wordpress.org/plugins/medical-before-after-gallery/) plugin is a complete gallery for up to 12 cases in 4 categories: a comparison slider with Slider, Before and After views, hover-reveal or side-by-side cards, four category styles, doctor cards and image cropping.

MBA Gallery Pro is an add-on to it. Here is what it adds, and where you can try each part.

## No limits

Pro removes the 12-case and 4-category limits. A case can also hold **several before/after pairs**, for more angles or sessions, each with its own label.

## Case windows and the progress timeline

The case window is where visitors look closely. Pro adds four window styles: **Cinema** (full screen), **Drawer**, **Comparison desk** with a linked magnifier, and **Case story**.

Every window can show a **progress timeline**: real, dated photos for each stage (6 weeks, 3 months, 1 year…). Photos are never blended or invented.

Try it: [Cinema window](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/window-cinema/) · [Case windows in the docs](/docs/case-windows/)

## Layouts, cards and templates

- **Seven more layouts**: Masonry, Spotlight, Procedure rows, Editorial, Angles, Uncropped and Collections.
- **Four more card styles**: slider, photo, before/after switch and stacked.
- **Six design templates** (Clinical, Modern, Luxury, Editorial, Compact and Photo-first), previewed with your own cases and applied in one click, with Undo.
- **Style presets and dark mode.**

Read more: [Layouts](/docs/layouts/) · [Design templates and preview](/docs/design-templates-and-preview/)

## Helping visitors find their case

- **Eight more category styles**, with 92 built-in icons or your own image.
- **Case filters**: gender, age range, recovery time, treatment duration, results visibility, procedure type, the doctor, and your own filters.
- **Treatment hub, concerns and doctor portfolios** as starting points, plus hand-picked **collections**.
- **Find by area** (a face, body and smile map) and **Help me choose** (up to three short questions of your own).
- **Compare two cases** side by side.

Read more: [Category styles and icons](/docs/category-styles-and-icons/) · [Case filters](/docs/case-filters/) · [Page structures and collections](/docs/page-structures-and-collections/)

## Cases on your treatment pages

`[mba_case]` puts one case, with its stages, on any page. `[mba_category_carousel]` shows a sliding row of one category's cases. `[mba_body_map]` puts the face and body map on a page of its own.

Read more: [Single-case embed](/docs/single-case-embed/) · [Category carousel](/docs/category-carousel/)

## Protecting the photos

- **Watermarks**: your text or logo on every gallery photo. Originals are never changed; the plugin saves watermarked copies.
- **Protect the originals** (optional): visitors can only open the watermarked copies, on Apache and LiteSpeed servers.
- **Sensitive-content notice**: photo by photo, each photo blurred with its own Show button, or a whole-page notice.

These tools help you publish with care. They do not replace written patient consent or legal advice.

Read more: [Watermarking](/docs/watermarking/) · [Consent and protection](/docs/consent-and-protection/)

## Upgrading

Install Pro next to the free plugin and activate your license; your existing cases and categories stay as they are. If you deactivate Pro, they remain, but the free plugin's limits of 12 cases and 4 categories apply again.
