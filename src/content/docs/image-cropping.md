The cropping tool is part of the **free** plugin. It keeps before and after photos the same size, so the comparison slider wipes cleanly and the grid looks even.

## Turn it on

The **Image Cropping** settings are in the gallery settings (with Pro: **Settings** in the workspace). Set the target **width** and **height**.

## Cropping a photo

When you add a photo to a case, the cropper opens so you can frame it to the set size. Confirm to save.

- Your **original upload is kept** — cropping saves a new copy and remembers the original.
- With Pro, the case editor can also **line up** the after photo with the before photo (saved as a copy too).

## Tips

- Frame the before and after photo of a pair the same way.
- Pro’s ready-to-publish checks warn when the two photos of a pair differ in shape by more than 10%, or a photo is under 600 px on its shorter side.
- Cropping needs the **GD** or **Imagick** image library — the same as [watermarks](/docs/watermarking/).
