The case window opens when a visitor chooses a case. Every style has the comparison (Slider, Before, After), the case details and the doctor card.

## Default window (Free & Pro)

The free plugin’s window shows a draggable slider with labelled **Slider**, **Before** and **After** views, a doctor card under the photo on wide screens, and arrows to move between cases without closing the window. A blurred copy of the photo fills the space around it instead of black bars — choose a plain background under **Design → Case Window Background**.

With Pro, the default window also shows a **progress timeline** under the photo for cases photographed over time.

## Pro window styles

Choose the style under **Design → Case window** (setting **Window style**). It applies to galleries and carousels.

| Style | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default** | Keeps the classic or masonry case window. |
| **Cinema** | A dark full-screen stage, a filmstrip and a details sheet. The controls rest when the mouse is still. |
| **Drawer** | Cases open in a side panel that keeps the gallery in view; a bottom sheet on phones. |
| **Comparison desk** | Before and after side by side with a linked magnifier and a compact facts rail. |
| **Case story** | A scrolling case study: comparison, treatment details and progress, ending in your booking button. |

**Story mode on phones** (its own switch) opens cases full screen on phones under 768 px, also when Default is selected: press and hold to see the before photo, swipe between cases.

### Viewer features

Each can be switched off in the same section:

- Show sharing controls
- Show progress timelines
- Show the “What to notice” tour (see [Image annotations](/docs/image-annotations/))
- Show doctor cards
- Show image thumbnails

Before/after labels, the starting view, case details, the booking button and shareable links are set in the same section — see [Comparison & labels](/docs/comparison-and-labels/).

## Progress timeline

Some results take months. With Pro, the **Progress timeline** box in the case editor adds a photo for each stage of a case — for example 6 weeks, 3 months and 1 year — up to 12 added stages. Reorder them with Move up / Move down.

- Visitors step from **Before** through every stage to **After** on a timeline under the photo, in every window style and in the [single-case embed](/docs/single-case-embed/).
- The photos are your real stage photos: nothing is blended or generated.
- Stage photos are watermarked like every other case photo.
- Cards of cases with recorded stages can show a **Progress** badge (Design → Gallery, card extras).

Try it: [default window with stages](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/progress-stages/) · [Cinema](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/window-cinema/) · [Drawer](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/window-drawer/) · [Comparison desk](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/window-comparison-desk/) · [Case story](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/window-case-story/).

## Keyboard

Escape closes the window. Alt + Left or Right Arrow changes case. On the slider, the arrow keys move the divider.
