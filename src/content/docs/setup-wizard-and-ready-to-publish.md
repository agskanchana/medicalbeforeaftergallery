## Setup wizard (Pro)

On a site with no cases yet, Pro offers **Set up your gallery**. It has five steps, and you can go back at any point:

1. **Practice type** — Dental, Plastic surgery, Dermatology & skin, Hair restoration, Med spa & aesthetics, Weight loss, or Other.
2. **Starter categories** — suggestions for your practice type to choose from. For example, Dental suggests Smile makeovers, Dental implants, Veneers, Teeth whitening and Orthodontics.
3. **Look** — one of the six [design templates](/docs/design-templates-and-preview/), each with a full-screen preview.
4. **Gallery page** — choose a page that already contains `[medbeafgallery]`, or create one as a draft or published page.
5. **Done**.

## Overview

The **Overview** of the Pro workspace shows:

- the gallery shortcode, with a copy button;
- the pages that show the gallery;
- counts of published cases, categories and gallery images;
- a getting-started checklist (create categories, add cases, put the gallery on a page) until those steps are done;
- the cases that need attention.

## Ready-to-publish checks

Pro checks every case and lists what to fix before it goes live:

- the main before or after photo is missing, or a photo is missing from the Media Library;
- a photo has no alt text;
- a photo is under 600 px on its shorter side;
- the before and after photos of a pair differ in shape by more than 10% (they may not line up);
- a photo still needs its watermarked copy;
- the case has no category, description or doctor.

The **Cases** screen gets a **Ready** column, so you can see at a glance which cases are ready to publish.
