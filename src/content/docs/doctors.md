## Doctors screen (Free)

Go to **Medical Before After Gallery → Doctors**. It is laid out as a staff directory: photo (or initials), credentials, location and case count on each card. Add and edit a doctor in a side panel, drag to reorder, and delete with a clear warning.

Each doctor has a name, photo, credentials, location and a profile link.

## Choose the doctor in each case

In the case editor, pick the doctor who treated the case. The case window then shows a **doctor card** — under the photo on wide screens.

## With Pro

- **Doctor on the cards** — turn on the doctor’s avatar and name under **Design → Gallery** (card extras).
- **Doctor filter** — visitors can filter the gallery by doctor.
- **Doctor cards in every case window** style.
- **Doctor portfolios** — a page structure where visitors pick a doctor and see that doctor’s treatments and results: `[medbeafgallery structure="doctors"]`. See [Treatment hub, concern first & collections](/docs/page-structures-and-collections/).

Doctors need free 1.5.0 or newer.

Try it: [doctors demo](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/doctors/) · [doctor portfolios](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/doctor-portfolios/).
