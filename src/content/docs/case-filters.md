Filters let visitors narrow the gallery to the cases that matter to them. They are a Pro feature, managed in the **Filters** section of the Pro workspace.

## Built-in filters

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Gender** | Female, Male |
| **Age Range** | 18-30, 31-45, 46-60, 60+ |
| **Recovery Time** | Minimal (1–3 days), Short (4–7 days), Moderate (1–2 weeks), Extended (2+ weeks) |
| **Treatment Duration** | Single Session, Multiple Sessions |
| **Results Visibility** | Immediate, Gradual |
| **Procedure Type** | Surgical, Minimally Invasive, Non-Surgical |

With free 1.5.0 or newer, visitors can also filter by **doctor**.

## Managing filters

- **Turn any filter on or off** (turned-off filters are kept, just hidden).
- **Edit the label and options**, and **reorder** filters.
- **Add your own filters** with your own label and options. A URL key is generated for shareable links.
- **Reset to defaults** restores the original six.

## Setting values per case

Open a case and set its values for each filter that is on. A case can match more than one value of a filter.

## On the page

Under **Design → Categories & filters**, choose whether the filters sit in a **sidebar** or open in a **drawer**. Visitors can combine filters; active choices show as removable tags with a clear-all control. Filters work together with [search and sort](/docs/search-sort-deep-linking/).

`[medbeafgallery show_filters="false"]` hides the category bar and the filters for one gallery.

The **Filter bar** category style puts treatment, doctor and recovery time in one row of drop-downs instead — see [Category styles](/docs/category-styles-and-icons/).

Try it: [case filters demo](https://demo.medicalbeforeaftergallery.com/pro-demos/custom-case-filters/).
